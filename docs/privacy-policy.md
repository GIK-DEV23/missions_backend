# Privacy Policy — GIK ("God In Kenya")

*Last updated: [date to be filled in on publication]*

This Privacy Policy explains how the GIK app ("we," "us," "the ministry") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the GIK mobile app or web admin. It is written to comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 ("the DPA").

## 1. Who this applies to

- **Missioners and app users** — people who use the app to record souls, testimonies, miracles, highlight moments, and mission reports.
- **Individuals recorded by missioners** — people reached at a mission event whose name, contact, and follow-up information is entered into the app by someone else on their behalf.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | What | From whom |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, email, role | App users (missioners/admins) at registration |
| Soul records | Name, phone number, age group, gender, status (Born Again / Yet to Receive Jesus / New Believer), location, consent flag | Entered by a missioner, about someone they met at a mission |
| Follow-up notes | Progress update content, dates, author | App users, about a soul they're following up with |
| Testimonies, miracles, highlight moments | Submitted content, optional photo, submitter identity (unless submitted anonymously) | App users |
| Usage/audit data | Login activity, actions taken in the app, IP address, device platform | Automatically, from app users |

We intentionally collect a phone number for a soul record without requiring it to be unique — the same person may be recorded more than once by different missioners or at different missions. When this happens, anyone recording that soul can see who else is currently following up with the same person, so the ministry doesn't accidentally duplicate outreach.

## 3. Why we collect it

- To support pastoral follow-up with individuals reached through mission events.
- To generate mission reports for ministry leadership and partner organizations.
- To coordinate follow-up between multiple missioners when more than one person is supporting the same individual.
- To keep the app secure (audit logs, account activity).

## 4. Consent

Where practical, a missioner records that they have the individual's consent to store their details for follow-up purposes at the time the soul record is created. Where the individual is a minor, consent should be obtained through a parent or guardian wherever practicable.

## 5. Anonymous submissions

Testimonies, miracles, and highlight moments can be submitted anonymously. When submitted this way, we do not store your identity against the submission — only a non-identifying technical token used solely to prevent spam/abuse, which is never linked back to your account or shown to anyone, including ministry admins. Anonymous submissions go through a review step before becoming public.

## 6. Who we share data with

- Ministry leadership and admins with an appropriate role, for follow-up coordination and reporting.
- We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing.
- Aggregate, de-identified statistics (e.g. total souls reached at an event) may be shared publicly or with partner organizations.

## 7. How long we keep it

Soul records are retained for a period defined by ministry policy based on follow-up activity; inactive records are archived or anonymized after that period. Audit logs are retained separately, for security purposes.

## 8. Your rights

If you are an app user, or an individual whose information was recorded by a missioner, you can request:
- A copy of the personal data held about you.
- Correction of inaccurate data.
- Deletion of your data, subject to legitimate record-keeping needs.

To make a request, contact [ministry contact email/phone to be filled in].

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and audit logging to protect personal data. Despite these measures, no system is completely secure, and we encourage you to safeguard your own account credentials.

## 10. Data breaches

If a breach occurs that risks your rights or freedoms, we will notify Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as required by law, and notify affected individuals where appropriate.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app.

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to [ministry contact email/phone to be filled in].
